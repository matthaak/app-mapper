# MD Editor

The Markdown editor (`src/modules/md-editor/`): one document shown two ways, **Visual** (rendered and editable in place) and **Raw** (the source), with tracked changes and comments. Demo at `/md-editor.html`.{++ The [Mapper](mapper.md) uses it, and it is the whole of the standalone [MD Editor App](md-editor-app.md).++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 15:24: Added: the standalone MD Editor app<<}

## How it works

* **The markdown is the single source of truth.** Every toolbar command is a transform of the markdown, so it does the same thing in either view, and undo works across both.
* **Switching views** carries the selection across exactly — Raw to Visual through the renderer's source map, Visual to Raw through sentinels in the serializer — and keeps the same text at the same height on screen.
* **Tracked changes**: additions {--`++…++`--}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 13:56: Changed: review marks are CriticMarkup<<}{++`{++…++}`++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 13:56<<} (underlined) and deletions {--`~~…~~`--}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 13:56<<}{++`{--…--}`++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 13:56<<} (struck through), which Accept and Reject resolve. As in Word, each author's changes are in that author's colour, additions and deletions alike, in both views (the Raw text too){++; in Raw a change's notes are in its colour too, and a highlight, its notes and an anchored comment are all highlighted in a light shade of their author's colour++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 15:24: Added: notes in their change’s colour in Raw<<}{>>Opus 2026-11-18 22:23: Updated: highlights and comments now take their author's colour too, in both views, and the highlight button shows yours<<}. Authors take the colours (`--md-author-1` to `-8`: red, blue, green, violet, teal, orange, magenta, grey) in the order they first made changes, so a colour stays with its author. With Track Changes on, typing becomes the user's additions and deleting their deletions, spaces included: typing on extends the addition at the caret, deleting from it really deletes, and deletions that touch merge into one. Accept and Reject apply to the changes the selection touches, or that the caret is in or right beside. A change with comments keeps its history: Accept or Reject adds the user's "Accepted" or "Rejected" to its thread (nothing, if {--it already ends that way--}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 18:55: Changed: compared with the verdict, wherever it is<<}{++that is its verdict already++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 18:55: Changed: compared with the verdict, wherever it is<<}; the other one flips it). {--A thread whose last comment is "Accepted" or "Rejected" (any case) marks the change resolved--}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 18:55: Changed: the verdict is the latest bare Accepted or Rejected, not the last comment<<}{++A change's verdict is its thread's latest note whose whole comment is "Accepted" or "Rejected" (any case, a final "." or "!" allowed); comments after it don't reopen it, and a later verdict replaces it. A change with a verdict is resolved++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 18:55: Changed: the verdict is the latest bare Accepted or Rejected, not the last comment<<}: Visual shows its outcome — the text that stays as plain text, the text that goes not at all (closing up the space it leaves) — and its review box says so; Raw shows the markup as ever. A change with no comments is settled for good: its markup and attribution go, and so does the text it takes out.{++ Deleting across paragraphs makes one deletion, running over the breaks between them: accepted, it joins what is left either side. Pasting several paragraphs makes one addition. Return at the end of a change starts the new paragraph clear of it (the browser would carry the change, attribution and all, into it).

## Files

* `editor.ts` — `MarkdownEditor`: the views, selection mapping, history and input.
* `parse.ts` and `serialize.ts` — Markdown to HTML with a source map, and the Visual DOM back to Markdown.
* `actions.ts`, `track.ts` and `notes.ts` — formatting commands, tracked editing and {--attribution threads--}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 13:56: Changed: review marks are CriticMarkup<<}{++notes++}{>>Claude 2026-11-18 13:56<<}.
* `review.ts` — the review column: its boxes, clusters and lines. `toolbar.ts` — the toolbar's tools and shortcuts. `hasRevisions(md)` says whether a text has additions, deletions or comments (the mapper turns Track Changes on for those). Given `onSave`, the editor shows a Save button (⌘S) at the left of its toolbar, enabled while `modified` is true; `flush()` reports an edit still waiting to be read back. `detachToolbar()` hands the toolbar to the host to show elsewhere; it still acts on its own editor.

## API

`new MarkdownEditor({ value, user, trackChanges, onChange })` mounts anywhere that gives it a size. `value` gets and sets the markdown (setting it clears undo); `onChange` reports edits (Visual edits after a short pause); `mode`, `trackChanges`, `exec`, `undo` and `redo` drive it from outside.
